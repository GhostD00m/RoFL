\section{Problem Statement}

Given the SRS over alphabet $\Sigma = \{a, b, c\}$:

\begin{align*}
&1.\ abbc \to cbba \\
&2.\ accb \to bcca \\  
&3.\ baac \to caab \\
&4.\ cb \to bc \\
&5.\ ca \to ab \\
\end{align*}

Determine: termination, finiteness of equivalence classes, local confluence, and Knuth-Bendix completability.

\section{Termination Analysis}

Let $M = \Sigma^*$ be the free monoid. Define the algebra:

\[
A = \langle M, F \rangle \quad \text{where} \quad F = \{\langle \varepsilon, 0\rangle, \langle a, 0\rangle, \langle b, 0\rangle, \langle c, 0\rangle, \langle ·, 2\rangle\}
\]

The operation $· : M \times M \to M$ is string concatenation.

Define the order $\succ$ via the strictly monotonic function $f: M \to \mathbb{N}$:

\[
f(w) = \left(|w|_c + \sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=i+1}^{n}I[s_i = a]I[s_j = c]\right)^{|w|_c \cdot |w|_b+1} + \sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=i+1}^{n}I[s_i = c]I[s_j = b]
\]

The order is defined as: \\
\[
u \succ v \Leftrightarrow 
\begin{cases}
f(u) > f(v) \\
\text{or} \\
f(u) = f(v) \land u >_{\text{lex}} v
\end{cases}
\]

where $>_{\text{lex}}$ is the lexicographic order with $a < b < c$.

Compute function values for all rules:

\begin{align*}
&f(abbc) = 8 > f(cbba) = 3 \\
&f(accb) = 66 > f(bcca) = 8 \\  
&f(baac) = 27 > f(caab) = 2 \\
&f(cb) = 2 > f(bc) = 1 \\
&f(ca) = 1 > f(ab) = 0
\end{align*}

All rewrite rules decrease in this order. By the Main Theorem on TRS Termination, $\langle A, \succ \rangle$ is a WFMA compatible with the SRS, hence the system terminates.

\section{Finiteness of Equivalence Classes}

The system has \textbf{infinitely many equivalence classes} with respect to normal forms because word length is preserved by all rewrite rules:

\begin{align*}
&|abbc| = |cbba| = 4 \\
&|accb| = |bcca| = 4 \\
&|baac| = |caab| = 4 \\  
&|cb| = |bc| = 2 \\
&|ca| = |ab| = 2
\end{align*}

Since length is invariant and there exist irreducible words of arbitrary length, there are infinitely many distinct equivalence classes.

\section{Local Confluence Analysis}

The system is \textbf{not locally confluent}. \\
Consider the critical pair $\langle (accb)[accb \to bcca], (accb)[cb \to bc] \rangle$:

\begin{align*}
&accb \to bcca \to  bcab \twoheadrightarrow babb\\
&accb \to acbc \twoheadrightarrow abcc
\end{align*}

The normal forms $bcca$ and $abcc$ are distinct and irreducible, violating the unique normal form property.
